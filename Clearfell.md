In Aldur league there are 15 TGRs. The layout don't seem to undergo rotations. Noprth layouts seems to have 2 variations of Beira position, and 1 south location also has 2 beira variations.

Diagrams are called with 3 letters each representing direction each zone entrance is facing, for example SWN
- S town
- W mud burrow
- N grelwood

The entrances are actually rotated by 45 degrees anticlockwise, so to get from actual direction to TGR name you have to rotate by 45 degrees:
- **N** =Northwest (NW)
- **S** = Southeast (SE)
- **E** = Northeast (NE)
- **W** = Southwest (SW

The first tell is town entrance direction:
- 6 S layouts
- 6 N layouts
- 3 W layouts
